    Meet Bellasofia

    The Creative Behind The Social Hour

    Hi, I'm Bellasofia, the founder and creative mind behind The Social Hour Agency. I'm a photographer and videographer capturing weddings, live events, sports, brand content and real estate across Philly + NJ.

    The Social Hour is a boutique content creation business built from my passion for storytelling, creativity and capturing life's most memorable moments. With a background in marketing, photography and digital communications, I help individuals, brands and businesses bring their visions to life through authentic, engaging content.

    My creative journey began with a camera and a curiosity for seeing the world through a different lens. From special celebrations and live events to social media content for businesses and sports organizations, I love finding the little details that make every story unique.

    Beyond the camera, I'm a certified Pilates instructor and a first-degree black belt in mixed martial arts. My background in fitness, athletics and movement has shaped my discipline, creativity and appreciation for the dedication behind every craft.

    I believe every moment has a story worth telling. My goal is to create content that feels genuine, intentional and uniquely yours, and to build meaningful relationships with my clients along the way.
