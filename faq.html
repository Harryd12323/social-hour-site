General

Where are you based, and do you travel?

I’m based in the Philadelphia and South Jersey area and shoot throughout Philly and South Jersey. If your shoot is somewhere else, reach out with the location and we can talk it through.

How do I book a shoot?

Fill out the contact form, or reach me by email or phone. Tell me what you’re looking for and your date, and I’ll get back to you with next steps.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, especially for weddings and engagements, since popular dates can book up. For real estate, events and brand shoots, reach out as soon as you know your date and I’ll let you know what’s available.

Do you require a deposit to hold my date?

Booking details, including how to hold your date, come with your quote. Get in touch and I’ll walk you through how it works.

Do you offer both photo and video?

Yes. I offer both photo and video, including reels and highlight videos. Let me know which you’re interested in when you get in touch.

How long until I receive my photos and videos?

It depends on the type of shoot and how much editing it needs. I’ll give you a delivery timeline when we plan your shoot.

How will my photos and videos be delivered?

Your edited photos and videos are delivered digitally. I’ll share exactly how you’ll receive them when we plan your shoot.

Weddings & Engagements

Do you photograph full wedding days, or engagements and proposals only?

I capture engagements, proposals and weddings, from the ring to the reception. Get in touch with your date and what you have in mind, and we’ll talk about the coverage that fits. See more on the Weddings & Engagements page.

Can you help plan and capture a surprise proposal?

Yes, I’d love to capture your proposal. Reach out with your idea, date and location, and we’ll plan how to catch the moment without giving the surprise away.

How many photos will we receive?

It depends on the type of session and how long it runs. We’ll go over what to expect when we plan your shoot.

Do you create wedding reels and highlight videos?

Yes, I create wedding reels and highlight videos. You can see a ceremony reel and a reception reel in the Videos section.

What should we wear to our engagement session?

Wear something that feels like you and that you’re comfortable moving in. If you’d like ideas on colors or coordinating outfits, just ask.

Real Estate

What types of properties do you photograph?

I photograph homes and commercial spaces, from family homes and condos to a medical office. You can see examples on the Real Estate page. If you have a different type of property, just ask.

How should the home be prepared before the shoot?

A clean, decluttered space photographs best: clear the counters, turn on the lights, open the blinds and put away personal items. I’m happy to go over anything specific to your property before the shoot.

Do you offer drone and aerial photos?

Aerial photos are part of my work, and you can see examples in the Aerial section of the gallery. Tell me about your property and I’ll let you know what’s possible for your listing.

How quickly are listing photos delivered?

Listings move fast, so ask about timing when you book and I’ll let you know when to expect your photos.

Do you shoot video walkthroughs or reels for listings?

I offer both photo and video. Ask about video for your listing when you get in touch, and I’ll let you know what I can put together.

Events & Brands

What kinds of events do you cover?

I cover concerts and live shows, sports, and special celebrations. If you have another kind of event in mind, get in touch. See more on the Events & Sports page.

Do you photograph concerts and sports?

Yes. I shoot concerts and DJ sets, from the barricade to backstage, and sports from courtside. You can see examples in the Events & Sports section of the gallery.

Can you create social media content for my brand?

Yes, I create reels and social content for local brands. You can see examples in the Videos section. Get in touch with what you have in mind.

Who owns the rights to brand content you create?

Usage rights depend on the project. We’ll go over how you can use your content before we start.

Can you deliver same-day or next-day highlights from an event?

Ask about quick-turnaround highlights when you book, and I’ll let you know what’s possible for your event.
